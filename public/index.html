Each owns one responsibility and hands off to the next.

01 Market Intelligence

Fetches OHLCV, order books, sentiment and on-chain data. An LLM classifies the prevailing market regime.

02 Strategy

Runs five strategies, regime-weights their signals, and uses an LLM to filter down to high-conviction trades only.

03 Risk Management

Kelly-adjusted position sizing, VaR check, drawdown guard and a hard kill switch.

04 Execution

TWAP and market order routing via CCXT, with slippage protection across major exchanges.

05 Portfolio Manager

Updates equity, tracks peaks, reinvests profits through compounding and detects rebalance needs.

06 Learning

Evaluates each cycle, adjusts strategy weights and stores memory in a Qdrant vector store.
